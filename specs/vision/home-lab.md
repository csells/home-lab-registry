# Home-lab vision

Make the computers we own a dependable platform for running the home, building
software, and exploring ideas—with minimal administration.

## A dependable home

Keep home automation, media, and everyday services dependable and protected from
development and experimentation.

## Autonomous agents

Give agents a persistent home and the ability to discover and acquire the resources
they need to complete the work we ask for.

## Build and test anywhere

Pool our compute for Bazel builds, GPU workloads, and testing across Linux, Windows,
macOS, iOS, and Android while preserving personal desktop and gaming use.

## Work that lasts as long as it needs

Let agents reuse environments for minutes or weeks, release resources when finished,
and resume from saved work with evidence accessible after an environment is gone.

## Shared capabilities

Provide speech, model inference, messaging, and other reusable services that agents
and applications can discover and use.

## One clear view

Give people and agents a shared, accurate view of what exists, what it does, who
owns it, how healthy it is, and what capacity is available.

## Access and recovery from anywhere

Make the lab easy to operate securely from a phone or computer, including recovery
when a machine or its normal access path fails.

## Low overhead, lasting confidence

Build on maintained open-source tools and share capabilities across projects to
keep the lab simple to operate.

Catch warnings before outages, preserve recoverable backups, and automate routine
operations so the lab returns more time and computing power than it consumes.

## Success

More useful work, faster feedback, dependable household services, and fewer manual
interventions as the lab grows.
